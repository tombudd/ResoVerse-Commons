# Project Governance

ResoVerse Commons begins with Founder stewardship by Tom Budd. Contributions
are developed in public, but merge, candidate acceptance, sandbox approval,
runtime activation, evidence admission, learning use, and production deployment
are separate decisions.

## Current roles

- **Contributor:** proposes code, fixtures, protocols, tests, or documentation.
- **Maintainer:** triages work, protects the public boundary, and decides
  whether a change is ready for independent review and merge.
- **Independent reviewer:** evaluates a candidate but must be distinct from the
  actor whose work is being reviewed.
- **Founder steward:** currently holds maintainer and project-direction
  responsibility.

No role may authorize itself beyond its declared authority. Mechanical test
success is evidence, not unrestricted approval.

## Decisions and appeals

Maintainers record `PASS`, `HOLD`, or closure with a concrete explanation.
Contributors may provide new evidence, narrow scope, or ask for reconsideration
in the issue or pull request. Security and conduct matters use their private
reporting paths.

## Evolution

The maintainer and reviewer model is expected to evolve as the contributor base
grows. Changes to project governance will be proposed publicly and recorded in
version control.

See [Governance Boundary](GOVERNANCE_BOUNDARY.md), [Maintainers and Registry
Trust](MAINTAINERS.md), and [Roadmap](ROADMAP.md).
